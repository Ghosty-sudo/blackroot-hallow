# Steam Store Draft — not final marketing copy

## Short description
Bind cursed Rootmarks, carve through a living underworld, and grow stronger between expeditions in a compact top-down pixel action-RPG where every power comes with a price.

## Feature pillars
- Power with consequences: every Rootmark grants a strong blessing and a real curse.
- Tight top-down combat built for keyboard/mouse and controller.
- Persistent town progression without endless grind.
- Three corrupted biomes, dangerous guardians, relic-driven builds, and a final descent into the Heartwood.
- A focused premium RPG designed to respect a player's time.

## Likely tags
Action RPG, Pixel Graphics, Top-Down, Dungeon Crawler, Dark Fantasy, Singleplayer, Indie, Roguelite, Action, Controller.

## Initial pricing hypothesis
$7.99–$11.99 USD pending content length, visual quality, demo response, comparable-title research, and wishlists. Do not lock price until near store-page readiness.
