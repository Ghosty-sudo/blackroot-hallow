# Blackroot Hollow — Game Design Lock v0.1

## Commercial pitch
A compact top-down pixel action-RPG where every build begins with a bargain. Bind a Rootmark for a major power spike and accept its curse, then descend into the living root network beneath a frontier town before the Heartwood consumes it.

## Product identity
- Single-player premium PC game.
- Dark-fantasy frontier tone without grimdark excess.
- Fast readable combat, short expedition structure, persistent town upgrades.
- 2–4 hour first ending; 6–10 hours for unlocks/challenge clears is the stretch target.
- Designed for keyboard/mouse and controller from the start.

## v1.0 scope ceiling
- 1 hub town.
- 3 distinct biomes + final Heartwood chamber.
- 4 weapon archetypes.
- 9 regular enemy archetypes.
- 4 bosses.
- 12 Rootmarks.
- 24 relics.
- 8 permanent town upgrades.
- 12–18 NPC dialogue states, not a sprawling quest tree.
- 1 main ending + 1 challenge ending if inexpensive enough.
- 12–20 Steam achievements.

## Core verbs
Move, dodge, attack, choose, risk, upgrade, descend, return.

## Differentiator
Rootmarks must never be simple bonuses. Every one creates a noticeable tradeoff that changes play. The curse is not flavor text; it changes decision-making.

## Scope exclusions for 1.0
No online multiplayer, crafting tree, open world, procedural item affix spreadsheet, romance system, voiced dialogue, base building, or live-service content.
