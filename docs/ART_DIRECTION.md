# Art Direction — Blackroot Hollow

## Visual thesis
Readable 16-bit-inspired frontier dark fantasy: warm human light above ground, damp organic corruption below. Pixel art should feel handmade and deliberate, not noisy or over-rendered.

## Base presentation
- 320×180 internal canvas.
- Nearest-neighbor texture filtering.
- Strong silhouettes readable at gameplay scale.
- Restrained palettes per biome; important attacks may break palette for readability.
- Character bodies should use a consistent pixel density and outline logic.

## Biomes
### Gloamgrove
Deep moss, wet timber, muted ochre. The first descent should still feel recognizably forest-like.

### Marrowroot
Pale root tissue, old bone, grey-brown earth. More cramped and anatomical without becoming gore-heavy.

### Embermold
Rust, coal, fungal orange, smoke-dark green. Highest visual heat before the final Heartwood.

### Heartwood
Near-black bark with unnatural gold/red sap. The final chamber should feel simpler and more iconic than the busier biomes.

## Character readability
- Player: pale coat/cloth against darker ground, one clear weapon silhouette.
- Standard enemies: one major silhouette feature each.
- Bosses: at least 2× standard enemy visual footprint plus distinct telegraphs.
- Friendly NPCs: warmer values than enemies.

## Animation minimums for 1.0
Player idle/walk/attack/dodge/hurt/death per weapon requirements; enemy idle/move/attack/hurt/death; bosses need telegraph/attack/recovery/death. Reuse is acceptable where silhouette and timing still read cleanly.
