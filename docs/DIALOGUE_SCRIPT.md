# Blackroot Hollow — Critical-Path Dialogue Draft v0.1

These are compact release-scope dialogue states. They are written to fit the game's small UI and should not stop combat. Required story information is attached to milestone returns and guardian clears.

## 01 — First arrival / Mara Venn
**MARA VENN**

"Three descent crews stopped answering. You're the Warden we have left."

"Pick a weapon. Bind a Rootmark. Bring back Amber."

"Bring yourself back first."

## 02 — Rootmark introduction / Old Fen
**OLD FEN**

"Don't call a Rootmark a blessing. Makes you careless."

"It moves a cost. That's all. Figure out where before the Hollow does."

## 03 — First upgrade / Tallow
**TALLOW**

"Root Amber takes an edge better than steel. Takes almost anything, really."

"Don't ask me why it's warm."

## 04 — First Briar Warden defeat / Rootvoice
**ROOTVOICE**

"YOU CUT THE SHAPE THAT ONCE CUT FOR YOU."

Environmental reveal: beneath the briars is an old Warden badge.

## 05 — Return after Briar / Old Fen
**OLD FEN**

"That badge wasn't grown there."

"I knew the man who wore it. Harl Venn. Good Warden. Bad singer."

"Don't let anyone call him a beast to make this easier."

## 06 — Mara after Briar
**MARA VENN**

"Harl was family. Before my time, but family."

"The records say his crew died in a collapse."

"The records can wait. The Rest can't. Keep going."

## 07 — First Marrow Bell defeat / Rootvoice
**ROOTVOICE**

"A BARGAIN BURIED IS STILL A BARGAIN."

Environmental reveal: old wall carving names the Heartwood as party to a covenant.

## 08 — Return after Marrow / Tallow
**TALLOW**

"Covenant. That's what the carving said?"

"My ledger calls this material a mineral. My hammer says the ledger's lying."

"Amber started pulsing when you came back."

## 09 — Fen after Marrow
**OLD FEN**

"My Rootmark used to whisper when I slept. Thought that was fear."

"Maybe something was trying to talk and we trained Wardens not to listen."

## 10 — First Ember Stag defeat / Rootvoice
**ROOTVOICE**

"YOU NAMED THE FIRE CLEANSING. WE REMEMBER RUNNING."

Environmental reveal: a charred order bears the seal of Warden's Rest and authorizes burning the root when Amber extraction fell.

## 11 — Return after Ember / Mara
**MARA VENN**

"The seal is real."

"My office has copies of every order worth remembering. Not that one."

"Someone decided forgetting was cheaper than admitting what we did."

## 12 — Fen after Ember
**OLD FEN**

"We kept asking why the Hollow hated us."

"Wrong question."

"Ask what it thinks we're still doing."

## 13 — Heartwood threshold / Rootvoice
**ROOTVOICE**

"WARDEN."

"YOU HAVE HEARD OUR DEAD."

"COME HEAR WHAT REMAINS."

This is the first time the Rootvoice addresses the player directly and coherently.

## 14 — Heartwood Sentinel introduction
**HEARTWOOD SENTINEL**

"You wear the bargain like a weapon. So did I."

"The Rest calls this place a wound. The Heartwood calls the Rest a hunger."

"Both are correct."

"Show me what kind of Warden learned the difference."

## 15 — Sentinel phase transition
**HEARTWOOD SENTINEL**

"BRIAR TAUGHT YOU COMMITMENT."

"BONE TAUGHT YOU DISTANCE."

"EMBER TAUGHT YOU MOTION."

"NOW LEARN RESTRAINT."

## 16 — Sentinel defeat
**HEARTWOOD SENTINEL**

"Good."

"Not because you won."

"Because you stopped believing victory was the same as truth."

## 17 — Main ending / The Measured Cut
**MARA VENN**

"Cut the extraction lines and the Rest loses half its Amber supply. Maybe more."

**OLD FEN**

"Then we'll learn to live poorer. Beats dying rich."

**TALLOW**

"I'll miss the impossible metal."

"I won't miss it breathing on my anvil."

**ROOTVOICE**

"MEASURE GIVEN. MEASURE RETURNED."

"THE ROAD REMAINS."

## 18 — Post-ending hub
**MARA VENN**

"The roots are quiet. Not dead. Quiet."

"Maybe that's what peace sounds like down there."

"If you descend again, do it because you choose to. Not because we lied and called it duty."
