# Blackroot Hollow — Critical Path Story Script v1.0

This file is implementation-facing. The Story Bible owns canon; this script owns the minimum launch presentation needed to communicate it.

## S00 — New game / Warden's Rest

**Trigger:** first hub visit, before first descent.

**MARA VENN**
Three crews went down. None came back clean enough to tell us why.

You're the Warden I have left.

Clear the three guardian seals. Find what stopped them. Bring back Amber if you can.

Bring yourself back first.

**OLD FEN**
A mark isn't armor. It's a door.

Remember doors open both ways.

**TALLOW**
And if the door bites, bring me what falls off it. Amber still spends.

**Objective:** Choose a weapon and Rootmark. Descend.

### Early Mara
- Sit down before you fall down. The Hollow can wait thirty seconds.
- You came back. Mission report starts after breathing.
- Lost Amber is cheaper than another Warden.

- Root-dragged? Means the mark found a road home.
- Losing a fight teaches plenty. Staying dead teaches less.
- The Hollow doesn't care what you meant to dodge.

- You lost Amber. I kept the anvil. Between us, we'll recover.
- Next time dodge the part that nearly killed you. Professional advice.

## Continuity checks

- Mara cannot know the First Burn truth before S12.
- Fen can suspect the guardian truth before S04 but does not have proof.
- Tallow does not accept that Amber is life-memory immediately after S08; his turn happens across S09–S13.
- Rootvoice grammar improves noticeably after S12 and becomes clear at S14.
- The Heartwood never claims innocence.
- The Sentinel fight tests access; it is not the Heartwood trying to murder the player after agreeing to talk.
- The Measured Cut preserves the original channel and cuts only forced extraction.
- Postgame Amber remains mechanically available through sustainable natural shedding.
